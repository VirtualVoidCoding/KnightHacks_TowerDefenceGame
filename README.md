🌸 Enchanted Defense: Blight vs. Bloom 🌿
# GemForestTowerDefenceGame

💡 Inspiration
It all started with a mix of caffeine, ambition, and a bit of nerves. As a team entering our very first hackathon, we wanted to create something that wasn’t just fun to play, but visually and mechanically driven by the theme. The eternal struggle between Blight and Bloom sparked our imagination. We wanted to give players a choice: do you nurture the resilient, numerous forces of nature, or do you command the heavy-hitting, destructive power of the corruption? That spark became Enchanted Defense.

🎮 What it does
Enchanted Defense is a top-down tower defense game showcasing a battle between two different sides: the natural Bloom and the toxic Blight. The main goal is to strategically place defensive towers to protect your central power crystal from waves of oncoming enemies.

• The Bloom Faction: Focuses on strength in numbers, rapid fire rates, and map-spreading synergy.

• The Blight Faction: Relies on slow, hard-hitting, high-damage single-target strikes and area-of-effect corruption.

Players strategically place towers, manage their resource pools, and try to survive increasingly difficult waves as the map dynamically reflects the balance of power between the beautiful Bloom and the decaying Blight.


🛠️ How we built it
We built the entire game from scratch during the hackathon using the Unity game engine and C# scripting.

• Gameplay Mechanics: Custom grid-placement logic for towers and a robust wave-spawning system.
• Pathfinding: Implemented dynamic waypoint navigation for enemy AI to traverse the map.
• Visuals & UI: Crafted a distinct color palette—vibrant greens and pinks for Bloom versus deep purples and sickly greens for Blight—to make the faction choice feel impactful.


🏆 Accomplishments that we're proud of
Our biggest accomplishment is simply crossing the finish line and submitting our very first hackathon project! Stepping into the competitive hackathon world was challenging, but we successfully managed our scope, overcame countless merge conflicts, and pushed a fully working game to Devpost before the buzzer. Turning an idea into a functional Unity game in just a weekend is a feeling we won't forget.

As first-timers and duo for the project, it was tough but incredibly rewarding. We gained hands-on experience with:

🚀 What's next for Enchanted Defense
We want to expand the universe we started building this weekend! Future updates will include a dual-campaign mode where your choices in one level affect the terrain of the next, more tower variants for both factions, and a fully polished soundtrack to truly immerse players in the battle for the enchanted realm.
