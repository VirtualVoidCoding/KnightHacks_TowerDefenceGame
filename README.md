# GemForestTowerDefenceGame

🌸 Enchanted Defense: Blight vs. Bloom 🌿

💡 Inspiration
It all started with a mix of Celsius Energy Drink, ambition, and a bit of nerves. As a team entering our very first hackathon, we wanted to create something that wasn’t just fun to play, but visually and mechanically driven by the theme. The eternal struggle between Blight and Bloom sparked our imagination. We wanted to give players a choice: do you nurture the resilient, numerous forces of nature, or do you command the heavy-hitting, destructive power of the corruption? That spark became Enchanted Defense.

🎮 What it does
What it does...in the future..?!? Enchanted Defense is a top-down tower defense game showcasing a battle between two different sides: the natural Bloom and the toxic Blight. The main goal is to strategically place defensive towers to protect your central power crystal from waves of oncoming enemies.

-The Bloom Faction: Focuses on strength in numbers, rapid fire rates, and map-spreading synergy.

-The Blight Faction: Relies on slow, hard-hitting, high-damage single-target strikes and area-of-effect corruption.

Players strategically place towers, manage their resource pools, and try to survive increasingly difficult waves as the map dynamically reflects the balance of power between the beautiful Bloom and the decaying Blight.


🛠️ How we built it

For this hackathon, we focused on establishing the visual identity and structural layout of the game. Instead of building the entire game from scratch, we implemented the basic functions of the layout and lighting to showcase our theme's atmosphere. We built using the Unity game engine and C# scripting.

To turn this prototype into a fully complete project post-hackathon, we plan on using:

- The Unity Game Engine: To tie our graphics, level layouts, and visual scenes into an interactive application interface.

- Pathfinding: Implemented dynamic waypoint navigation for enemy AI to traverse the map.
- C# Scripting Layout Structures: To program our grid-placement logic for tower installations, wave-spawning mechanics, and custom enemy tracking loops.

- Lighting & Art Assets: To expand on our high-contrast layout, contrasting vibrant greens and pinks for the Bloom with deep purples and sickly greens for the Blight.
